3. **P1 annex — Mutation-command timeouts leave descendants running.**  
   [`.claude/scripts/mutant_sandbox.py:133`](/private/var/folders/9n/hm9srl0s1nz45_p12tj61pd80000gn/T/repass-rc1.3JOeTU/wt/.claude/scripts/mutant_sandbox.py:133), [cleanup at :147](/private/var/folders/9n/hm9srl0s1nz45_p12tj61pd80000gn/T/repass-rc1.3JOeTU/wt/.claude/scripts/mutant_sandbox.py:147). When a test command launches workers, `subprocess.run(timeout=…)` kills its direct child without terminating the descendants. A read-only process probe reproduced a surviving descendant after timeout. The helper then removes the worktree beneath those processes; repeated retries can accumulate workers and interfere with subsequent verification.  
   **Minimal fix:** isolate command execution in a process group and terminate/wait for its descendants before removing the sandbox.
