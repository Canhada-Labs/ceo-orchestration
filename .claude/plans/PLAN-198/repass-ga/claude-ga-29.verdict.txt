CARRIED P1 (already declared in the lane notes as claude-p08 and claude-p28; still present in the GA candidate, not new)
P1, .claude/hooks/_lib/_installer_write_safety/__init__.py:36
- Cause: _bind_class clones each class with original.__bases__. The entry point's Incomplete is therefore built on the original syntax.ParseError, not on the entry point's cloned ParseError (syntax.py:6-11), so issubclass(Incomplete, ParseError) is False.
- Effect: the rebound `except ParseError` handlers no longer catch Incomplete. Confirmed at operands.py:33-37 (canon_operand); the same applies to commands.py (_expansion_refs), model.py (extract_cmd_substs, _subst_commands, _token_is_expanded) and verdicts_b.py (parse_sed_script, _sole_pipeline_producer).
- Failure scenario: `dd "of=$dst"` in a scanned script makes the destination token `$dst"`. canon_operand then raises an Incomplete that nothing catches, and the census crashes with a traceback and exit 1, where v1.4.3-rc.1 reported a site.
- This also makes CHANGELOG.md:112 ("Entry points keep their paths and their observable contracts") untrue for this entry point.
- Minimal fix: in bind(), keep an original-to-clone map and rebuild bases as tuple(clones.get(b, b) for b in original.__bases__), or do not clone BaseException subclasses. Add a test that issubclass(mod.Incomplete, mod.ParseError) and a `dd "of=$x"` fixture.
