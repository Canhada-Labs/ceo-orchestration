P2-1 (new): the budget-bypass quota count goes stale under the bounded exit.
- Files: check_budget.py:635-669, with :930-931 and :790-818.
- Mechanism:
  - _count_recent_bypasses counts budget_bypass_used events in the canonical log through iter_events.
  - The same registered hook writes that event through the spool (_audit_emit_parts/writer.py:582-591).
  - lifecycle.py:348 skips the exit drain, so the event stays in the spool.
- Failure scenario:
  - With CEO_BUDGET_BYPASS=1, every bypassed spawn reports "BUDGET BYPASS USED ... (1/10 in 24h)".
  - The over-quota message never appears until a process outside the shim drains the spool. Each such drain moves at most the 100 oldest entries (the K_MAX break in drain.py).
- Impact: advisory only. In State 0, decide() always allows (check_budget.py:750). This is the same class as the carried overlap-rail P1, but neither condition 8 nor the CHANGELOG names it.
- Fix: apply the carried P1's fix (have readers also scan state/audit-spool.*.jsonl and *.draining.*, bounded and read-only, or write these governance events synchronously). Alternatively, declare in Known-open that readers of the canonical log are degraded.
