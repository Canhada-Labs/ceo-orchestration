NEW FINDINGS (annex)
P1 — .claude/hooks/_lib/_spool_writer_parts/lifecycle.py:346-357 together with prepare.py:83-123 and agent_spawn/file_assignment.py:327-380 (_recent_file_assignments). The spawn FILE ASSIGNMENT overlap rail no longer sees earlier spawns.
- Mechanism: under the bounded exit, a hook process never drains its spool.
  - The atexit and signal drains are skipped.
  - should_drain() is evaluated right after spool_append has fsynced, so the spool's mtime is about now and the 100 ms staleness trigger cannot fire on APFS or ext4. The only remaining trigger is 100 or more body lines in that one process's spool.
  - So the canonical log receives hook events only when a non-hook process (a script) exits or a drain is run by hand. Each such drain processes at most K_MAX=100 entries, oldest first (prepare.py _phase3_collect_and_sort sort; drain.py K_MAX break).
- Failure scenario: an adopter sets CEO_SPAWN_OVERLAP_GUARD=1.
  - Spawn A declares "CAN edit: src/x.py". Its hook writes spawn_file_assignment_recorded into audit-spool.<pid>.jsonl and exits without draining.
  - Two minutes later, spawn B declares the same path. _recent_file_assignments tails only the canonical audit log, finds nothing, and B is allowed.
  - The advisory overlap counts (the measure-first denominator for flipping this rail) are under-counted the same way.
  - In v1.4.3-rc.1, A's forced atexit drain had put its record in the log before B ran.
- Declared or not: the CHANGELOG ("can reach the log later"; Known-open "not proven") and condition 8 declare the delay itself. They do not declare that governance readers of the canonical log go blind meanwhile. The miss is in the fail-open direction; no legitimate session is blocked.
- Minimal fix: have _recent_file_assignments also scan, read-only and bounded, the state-dir audit-spool.*.jsonl and *.draining.* files for in-window spawn_file_assignment_recorded rows. Alternatively, record that event through a direct writer. In either case, add the rail's degradation to Known-open.
