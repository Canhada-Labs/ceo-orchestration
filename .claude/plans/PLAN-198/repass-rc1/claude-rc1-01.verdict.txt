3. P2: install() fails silently on non-function callables.
   - Where: .claude/hooks/_lib/_audit_emit_parts/__init__.py:21-48.
   - Problem: install() rebinds only FunctionType. A future part that exports a class, a functools-decorated callable or a partial would keep its template module's globals. It would then bypass facade monkeypatches, or raise NameError on facade-only names.
   - Today none of the three users (audit_emit, check_agent_spawn with _lib.agent_spawn, lessons.py with _lib._lessons_parts) exports one.
   - Minimal fix: raise TypeError in install() for any exported callable that is not a FunctionType, so such a part fails in tests instead of at runtime.
