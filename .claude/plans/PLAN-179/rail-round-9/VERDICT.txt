- [P2] Keep the flock inode alive during store collection — /Users/joaocanhada/.w179-rail9/repo/.claude/hooks/_lib/scratchpad_lib.py:715-718
  Under concurrent session activity, another writer may already have opened this lock file and be waiting on its flock. Unlinking the pathname while holding the lock lets a later process create and lock a different inode, after which both writers can enter their critical sections; because the state store opens SQLite before acquiring this lock, the GC can also remove a database already opened by the waiter, losing or failing its snapshot write.
