- [P1] Do not unlink stores while SQLite handles can be open — /Users/joaocanhada/.w179-rail10/repo/.claude/hooks/_lib/scratchpad_lib.py:711-716

The writer can then fail or commit into an unlinked database, losing the continuity snapshot, so deletion must be coordinated with database opening rather than only the later read/write lock.
